What it does

Less status chasing, more finished work.

  • Boards that stay tidy

    Columns, owners and due dates on one screen. Stale cards are flagged after 5 days so nothing quietly rots.

  • A weekly report in one click

    Every Friday, Fjordly drafts what moved, what slipped and why, straight from the board. Edit it and send it.

  • Recurring work, handled

    Weekly reviews, month-end checklists and on-call rotas create themselves on schedule.

  • Digests, not pings

    One message in Slack or email at the time you choose, instead of a notification for every comment.
