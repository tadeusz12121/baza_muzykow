Pricing questions

Something else? Ask our team.

Who counts as a member?

Anyone who can create or edit tasks. Guests who only view or comment on shared boards are free, up to 3 per member.

What happens when the trial ends?

You choose a plan or stay on Free. If you have more than 10 members, the extra people move to view-only until you upgrade. Nothing is deleted.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly?

Yes, at any time. Moving to yearly takes effect right away and we credit what is left of the month.

Do you offer discounts?

Nonprofits and schools get 50% off Team. Write to us from your organization’s email address.

How do I pay?

By card on Team. Enterprise can also pay by invoice with 30-day terms.
