Customer story · Northbeam Logistics

How Northbeam cut its Monday meeting from 90 minutes to 25.

A freight company with four hubs in Norway and Sweden moved its operations team from spreadsheets and email onto Fjordly in six weeks.

The problem: a status meeting that ate Monday

Northbeam’s operations team tracked work in a shared spreadsheet per hub. Every Friday, four hub leads spent about three hours turning those sheets into a status email, and on Monday 14 people met for 90 minutes to go through it line by line.

What changed

Each hub got a board with the same four columns. Recurring work, like dock audits and driver onboarding, became recurring tasks. On Fridays the hub leads now review the report Fjordly drafts, fix a sentence or two and post it to #ops.

The result

The Monday meeting now covers only the tasks marked at risk: 25 minutes, with 6 people instead of 14. Writing the weekly report takes each lead about 20 minutes. Northbeam rolled Fjordly out to finance and hiring next, and now has 18 boards.
