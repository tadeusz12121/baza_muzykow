Changelog

What’s new in Fjordly

New features, improvements and fixes. We ship every Tuesday.

Get updates by email
  1. November 4, 2026v3.8
    New

    Weekly report templates

    Pick a template for leadership, team or client reports. Each one sets the sections, the tone and which boards to include. You can save your own, too.

    • Three built-in templates
    • Save any report as a template
    • Choose boards per template

    Weekly report · Nov 2–6

    Draft

    Operations: 14 tasks done, 2 at risk

    • Peak season staffing plan signed off.
    • New driver onboarding live at all 4 hubs.
    • Route planner v2 slips to Nov 10: waiting on map data.
    Send to #opsEdit
  2. October 28, 2026v3.7.2
    Improved

    Boards with 500+ cards open twice as fast

    We now load the visible columns first and the rest as you scroll. A 1,200-card board opens in 0.9 seconds, down from 2.1.

  3. October 21, 2026v3.7
    New

    Recurring tasks

    Set any task to repeat daily, weekly, monthly or on the last workday of the month. The next one is created when the current one is done, or on schedule.

    • Skip one occurrence without breaking the series
    • Recurring checklists keep their items
  4. October 14, 2026v3.6.4
    Fixed

    Due dates in the right time zone

    Tasks due at midnight could show the previous day for people west of UTC. Due dates now follow the time zone of the person who set them.

  5. October 7, 2026v3.6
    New

    Slack and Teams digests

    Post a daily or weekly summary of any board to a channel. Mentions and blocked tasks still notify people right away.

    • Choose the time and the boards
    • Quiet hours per person
  6. September 30, 2026v3.5.1
    Improved

    More keyboard shortcuts

    Press ? anywhere to see them all. New: G then B to go to boards, Shift+D to clear a due date.
