Before you book

Do I need a demo to start?

No. Most teams start on Free and never talk to us. The demo is for teams over 50 people or anyone who needs Enterprise.

Can you help us move from another tool?

Yes. Imports from Trello, Asana, Jira and CSV are built in, and on Enterprise we run the migration with you.

Where is our data stored?

In the EU (Frankfurt) by default. Enterprise customers can choose the US (Oregon) instead.

Do you sign DPAs and security questionnaires?

Yes. Our DPA is ready to sign, and we answer security questionnaires for Team and Enterprise customers.
